# France ctc documents
From https://www.impots.gouv.fr/specifications-externes-b2b
The external specifications comprise all documents describing the formats and procedures for exchanging information with the tax authorities as part of the general rollout of electronic invoicing between VAT-liable businesses: on the one hand, with the public invoicing portal for transmitting regulatory data, and on the other, with Chorus Pro (Chorus Pro External Specifications: https://portail.chorus-pro.gouv.fr/aife_documentation?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0011471), the reference platform for electronic invoicing in the public sector.
These documents describe the public invoicing portal’s directory and reporting services, as well as the collection and processing by the authorities of invoicing, transaction, and payment data transmitted by approved platforms. They also describe the services offered by the public-sector platform and the handling of use cases involving a public buyer or customer.
The components of the common framework for businesses, compatible solutions, and approved platforms—in their interactions with one another and with compatible solutions—are detailed in the following documents issued by the standardization committee organized by the French Standards Association (AFNOR), published on the AFNOR website:
- Formats and profiles (invoice messages and lifecycle status messages): standard XP Z12-012;
- APIs for connecting companies’ information systems with approved platforms: standard XP Z12-013;
- B2B use cases: standard XP Z12-014.

Earlier versions of the external specifications (prior to v3.2), which covered all data flows, remain available for consultation.
As a reminder, the tax authorities contribute to and help coordinate this work, while ensuring that the reform remains balanced and meets its intended objectives.
